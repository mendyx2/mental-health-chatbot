# Alcohol, drugs and mental health

Alcohol and other substances can seem to relieve stress or sadness for a short time, but they usually worsen anxiety, low mood and sleep, and can lead to dependence.

## Signs that substance use may be a problem
Using more than planned, craving it, failing to cut down, neglecting studies, work or family, using to cope with feelings, needing more to get the same effect, or feeling unwell when stopping.

## Where to start
Notice when and why you use. Tell one trusted person. Avoid places and situations that trigger use, and replace them with other activities and support. Do not stop suddenly after heavy long-term use of alcohol or some drugs without medical advice, because withdrawal can be dangerous.

If someone has taken a large amount of a substance, is unconscious, very drowsy, having trouble breathing, or seems confused or having a seizure, this is an emergency. Call 112 or go to the nearest hospital immediately.

Recovery is possible, and many people get better with support from health professionals, family and community.
