# Crisis and safety information

## If you or someone else is in immediate danger
Call 112 (Nigeria's national emergency number) or go straight to the nearest hospital emergency unit. If you can, tell a trusted person near you and stay with them. Do not stay alone.

## Warning signs that need urgent attention
Talking about wanting to die or to end one's life, talking about hurting oneself, feeling like a burden or having no reason to live, giving away belongings, saying goodbye, sudden calm after deep despair, thoughts of harming other people, or being unable to stay safe.

## If you are having thoughts of suicide or self-harm
These thoughts are more common than people think, and they can pass, especially with support. You do not have to face them alone. Right now: tell someone you trust how you feel, move away from anything you could use to hurt yourself, and go to or call emergency services. Intense feelings often rise and fall, and getting through the next hour safely matters.

## How this chatbot responds
When a message suggests a person may be at risk, the chatbot will show emergency information and encourage contacting emergency services and a trusted person. It cannot call anyone for you.
