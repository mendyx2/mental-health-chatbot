# Relaxation and grounding exercises

## Slow breathing (about 3 minutes)
Sit comfortably. Breathe in through your nose for 4 seconds. Pause for 2 seconds. Breathe out slowly through your mouth for 6 seconds. Repeat. Place a hand on your belly and feel it rise and fall.

## 5-4-3-2-1 grounding (about 2 minutes)
Name 5 things you can see, 4 things you can touch or feel, 3 things you can hear, 2 things you can smell, and 1 thing you can taste. This brings attention back to the present moment.

## Progressive muscle relaxation (about 10 minutes)
Starting with your feet, tighten the muscles for 5 seconds, then release for 10 seconds and notice the difference. Move up through legs, stomach, hands, arms, shoulders and face. Breathe slowly throughout.

## Body check and release
Notice where you hold tension (jaw, shoulders, hands). Drop your shoulders, unclench your jaw and open your hands. Repeat a few times a day.

## A short pause for hard moments
Stop. Take one slow breath. Notice what you are feeling without judging it. Choose one small next step.

These skills work best when practised regularly when you are calm, so they are easier to use when you are stressed.
