# Where to find help

This chatbot is a demonstration and is not linked to any hospital or clinic. It does not have verified opening hours, phone numbers or booking details for any service, and it should not invent them.

## What to do for non-emergency help
Visit the nearest hospital, teaching hospital, primary health centre or a licensed doctor, psychologist or counsellor and ask for mental health support. Ask a trusted person to go with you if that helps.

## Emergencies
Call 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency unit.

If you ask this chatbot for a specific clinic's hours, location or phone number, explain that you do not have verified details and suggest contacting the facility directly.
