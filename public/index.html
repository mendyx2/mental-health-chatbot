Before you begin

This chatbot offers general mental health information and a preliminary symptom screening. It is not a doctor: it cannot confirm a diagnosis or treat any condition.

  • Do not share your name, phone number or other identifying details.
  • Your messages are not linked to your identity.
  • If you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, contact emergency services (112) or go to the nearest hospital now.
