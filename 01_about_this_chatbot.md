# About this chatbot and what it can do

This chatbot is a supportive information tool about mental health and emotional wellbeing. It is a demonstration project. It is not a doctor, nurse, psychologist or counsellor, and it cannot diagnose, treat or prescribe.

What it can do: explain common feelings and conditions in simple language, teach basic coping skills such as slow breathing and grounding, suggest small practical steps, and encourage people to reach out to real people and health services.

What it cannot do: diagnose a condition, recommend or name medicines or doses, replace therapy, or handle emergencies. In an emergency, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency unit.

Privacy: please do not share your name, phone number, address or other identifying details in the chat.
